About RoxAI

RoxAI: Built for Africa

Africa is a continent of talent, ambition, and untapped potential. RoxAI exists because we believe that when you uplift the institutions closest to home, the impact ripples outward — starting with Kenya, and reaching schools across the continent.

Here's the gap we saw: African and international schools have real budgets, real ambition, and real appetite for modern tools — but most AI and automation companies build for generic Western businesses, not for how schools actually run. Term structures, parent communication culture, local payment and connectivity realities — none of it is built in. So schools either go without, or adopt tools that don't quite fit.

We started with admin, specifically, because time is the one resource school staff can never get back. Every hour spent manually tracking fee payments, re-entering the same data, or chasing parent replies is an hour not spent on the students and families the school exists for. Automating that work isn't about replacing people — it's about giving schools back the time to focus on what actually grows them, including the relationships that help them attract and retain more students.

RoxAI was built to close that gap — practical, affordable automation, designed around how African and international schools actually operate, starting with the work that frees up the most time, fastest.
